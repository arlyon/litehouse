# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Litehouse serves home operators who want dependable automation without
administering a large software stack, advanced operators who manage several
homes and explicit permissions, and integration authors who build small typed
WebAssembly components. They move between the public website, documentation,
Cockpit control plane, automation editor, and local operational surfaces. The
system must remain approachable without concealing technical truth.

## Product Purpose

Litehouse is a lightweight, component-based home automation platform. It lets
people install integrations, compose typed services, create automations,
inspect failures, and operate paired servers with less startup, dependency,
deployment, and recovery complexity than incumbent platforms. Success means
the public story and daily control interface feel like one trustworthy system
at different information densities.

## Brand Personality

Quietly capable, direct, and trustworthy. Litehouse should communicate
technical depth through clarity and responsive behavior rather than visual
theatrics. Its voice is concise and candid, with enough warmth for a household
product and enough precision for infrastructure.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble generic enterprise SaaS: interchangeable KPI-card dashboards,
sales-led polish, decorative analytics, nested card grids, or admin-template
styling that could belong to any product. Avoid visual claims of sophistication
that are not backed by useful state or action.

## Design Principles

1. **One system, two densities.** Public pages and operational interfaces share
   one visual vocabulary; marketing breathes while Cockpit compresses.
2. **Capability before decoration.** Emphasize what a person can inspect,
   decide, or control. Ornament never competes with live state.
3. **Reveal technical truth progressively.** Start with plain language and clear
   status, then expose identifiers, contracts, traces, and diagnostics on
   demand.
4. **Calm under failure.** Errors are specific, recoverable, and visually
   steady. Urgency comes from meaning, not noise.
5. **Local trust is visible.** Pairing, permissions, direct connections, and
   offline behavior are explained at the point of action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA across public and authenticated surfaces. Every workflow must
support keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic structure, reduced motion,
non-color state cues, adequate target sizes, and AA text/non-text contrast.
Dense operational views must remain zoomable and screen-reader navigable
without hiding essential state.
